# Worked sector lenses

Five filled versions of [`lens_template.md`](../lens_template.md). **Pick the one closest to your sector, copy it, and replace the specifics** — the structure is what makes audits consistent, not the content.

Each was structurally abstracted from real material: identifying details are replaced with placeholders, and publicly-distributed sources are cited with attribution inside each file.

Two of the five are **not fundraise decks at all** — the M&A teaser and the structurer proposal invert most fundraise conventions (no TAM, no team slide, no problem/solution arc). If your artifact is one of those, starting from a fundraise lens will actively mislead you.

| Lens | The artifact it audits | The structural fact that drives everything else |
|---|---|---|
| [`credit-fintech.md`](credit-fintech.md) | Credit-fintech raising a **credit-instrument equity round** — investor equity funds the first-loss layer of a debt vehicle (FIDC, ABS facility, sub-quota), senior tranches stacked on top. | The deck is not pitching generic growth equity; it is pitching **the equity that makes senior-tranche leverage possible**. Every slide either supports or undermines that. |
| [`hard-tech.md`](hard-tech.md) | Hard-tech / energy-infrastructure raise with physical assets — mining infrastructure, data centres, renewables, grid hardware, manufacturing. | **Lead with the physical unit, not the financial abstraction.** Investors need kWh economics, PPA pricing and the country energy macro before any revenue projection means anything. |
| [`regulated-structured-product.md`](regulated-structured-product.md) | Brazilian regulated structured-product marketing — FIDCs, Fiagros, CRIs, CRAs, FIIs distributed to qualified institutional investors under **CVM 160**. | Not a pitch that the category is real. The allocator already knows the asset class; the question is whether **this offering, this manager, this moment** deserves allocation. The rubric carries compliance checkpoints alongside content quality. |
| [`structurer-proposal.md`](structurer-proposal.md) | A DCM firm, securitization desk or structured-credit boutique **pitching an issuer** to mandate their services for a transaction. | The issuer already wants capital-markets access. The proposal answers a different question: **why mandate this firm, and what does the transaction look like?** |
| [`m-and-a-teaser.md`](m-and-a-teaser.md) | Sell-side **blind teaser** — the short anonymized document an advisor circulates to test buyer interest before an NDA is signed. | Every convention inverts: shorter not longer, advisor-fronted not founder-fronted, anonymized not branded. The cover is the **only** impression management gets before the NDA gate. |

## Using one

1. Copy the closest file into your own project and rename it.
2. Replace every company-specific detail. Keep the section structure and numbering — the playbook cross-references sections by number.
3. Treat §9 (objection bank) as living. Add every hostile question a real meeting produces.

**They go stale in predictable places.** Comparable raises and transactions (§10) date fastest. In `hard-tech.md` the energy macro needs a refresh roughly every six months; in `regulated-structured-product.md` the cronograma and disclaimer language need review with counsel whenever the offering window or the regulation moves; in `m-and-a-teaser.md` the anonymization needs re-checking on every draft, because identifying specifics accumulate across versions.
